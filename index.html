Train With Me

Three ways to work together

Same programming, same accountability — pick the format that fits your schedule, your budget and how much hands-on coaching you want.

1-on-1 in person

My full attention for the whole session. Hands-on cueing, form corrected in real time, and the load adjusted set by set based on what I'm watching — not what a spreadsheet predicted. The fastest way to build technique you can trust.

Small group

Train alongside two to four other people — friends, a couple, coworkers. You still get a program written for you and eyes on your lifts, at a lower cost per session. The group is its own accountability: it's harder to skip when someone's waiting on you.

Online coaching

Not local, or you train on your own schedule? Your program lands in SemperfitGO, I see every set you log, and I adjust from there. Check-ins, form-video review and direct access to me — the plan just travels with you instead of meeting at a gym.

Not sure which fits? Start with the questionnaire and we'll figure it out on the call — plenty of people mix formats, like in person once a week and online the rest.
