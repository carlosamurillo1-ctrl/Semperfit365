What You Get

Coaching that actually follows you out of the gym

No generic PDFs. No guessing what weight to use. A real plan, adjusted as you progress, with me in your corner the whole way.

A program built for you

Designed around your goals, your training days, your injuries and the equipment you can actually get to — whether that's a full gym or a rack in the garage.

Progression you can see

Every set you log is saved week over week. Last week's numbers sit right there while you train, so adding weight is a decision, not a guess.

Nutrition that fits your life

Calorie and macro targets you can actually hit, tracked in the same app you train with — barcode scanner included, no second subscription.

Direct line to your coach

Questions between sessions, a form check, a week that went sideways — text or email me. You're not submitting a ticket, you're talking to your trainer.

Adjusted as you go

I can see what you're logging and push an updated program straight to your app. Your plan changes when your progress does — not three months later.

Accountability, honestly

The plan only works if you run it. I'll hold you to it — that's the part you're really paying for, and the part that gets people results.
